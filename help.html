09.AVolunteer

Give some time.

The centre is run by volunteers, and new volunteers are always welcome. Ring to find out what help is needed at the moment and how to get started.

The centre is also governed by a volunteer committee of 5 to 12 people. If you would like to know more about the committee, ask us.

Not ready to adopt but could look after an animal for a while? Ask about fostering.

Ring about volunteering

09.BDonate

Give a gift.

Donations help the centre care for animals. This website does not take payments: get in touch and the team will tell you how to give.

In person

At the centre

4 Seafield Road, Netherby, Ashburton. The centre is volunteer-run, so please call before visiting.

Find us

Ask us about a donation receipt

In New Zealand, people who pay tax can usually claim a tax credit of one third of their donations of $5 or more to approved donee organisations, which include many charities (Inland Revenue: about donation tax credits). Ask us about a receipt and whether your gift qualifies.

Where the money goes

The centre's income and spending for every year since 2018, from its annual returns, are on our About page. In the year to 31 December 2025, income was $9,142 and expenditure $46,545.

Give in crypto

The centre takes gifts in Solana and Ethereum. Scan a code or copy an address, and send on the matching network. A gift sent on the wrong network can be lost and can't be refunded.

Peaks

Found an animal?

The centre is a small volunteer-run charity, not an emergency service. For an animal in danger or distress, contact the SPCA, and for a wandering dog, Ashburton District Council.
